# Model Vocabulary Addendum

Status: Draft  
Repository: `civic-affordance-model`  
Scope: Civic Affordances Diagnostics

## Purpose

This addendum defines vocabulary used across the model documents. It is the
preferred place for terms that would otherwise be repeated in every doctrine,
intake, or interface file.

## Model, Not Product

Civic Affordance Diagnostics is a model.

It is not a product, SaaS platform, vendor package, franchise, centralized
application, or single-stack deployment.

A reference implementation, deployment playbook, or county root may publish
specific operational choices. Those choices do not become prerequisites for
other county roots.

## Civic Infrastructure

Civic Infrastructure is infrastructure used to surface, inspect, record, route,
publish, validate, federate, compare, or preserve civic affordances.

It is wiring, not purposing. It may enable a local actor to route mail, publish
records, federate channels, expose DNS, or validate an alias. Those technical
functions do not make the cooperative network responsible for every use made of
that infrastructure.

## Civic Affordance

A civic affordance is an evidenced capacity of a person, household, address,
role, local actor, professional, or qualified contributor to claim affected
status in relation to a civic condition.

A civic affordance is not merely an opinion, interest, preference, political
position, or social identity. It must have an affected-status claim and some
possible evidence anchor.

## Diagnostic Surface

A diagnostic surface is a structured place where a civic affordance can be
inspected, evidenced, recorded, discussed, routed, or published under defined
qualification rules.

A diagnostic surface may be public, authenticated, county-local, federated,
expert-gated, private, archive-only, IPFS-published, or otherwise constrained.

## County Root

A county root is the local owner-operator or operator group that controls a
declared county-state surface.

The term `root` identifies the operational root for that surface. It does not
mean authority over other counties, other roots, or the cooperative network.

## County-State Surface

A county-state surface is a declared civic infrastructure surface for a specific
county and state.

The surface may exist under any domain controlled by the contributor.

Examples:

```text
kane-il.us
orange-ca.ankerpoint-sawmill.com
dupage-il.example.org
```

The parent domain identifies the contributor's naming root. The county-state
label identifies the civic surface being contributed.

A county-state surface is not a monopoly claim over the county. Multiple roots
may declare surfaces for the same county.

## Cooperation Interface

A cooperation interface is a declared technical or administrative boundary
through which one root may cooperate with another.

Examples include:

- public ingress;
- DNS;
- Fediverse;
- directory / LDAP;
- Email / CEAS;
- WireGuard;
- DANE;
- attestation / durable publication.

## Cooperative Recognition

Cooperative recognition means that one or more roots elect to resolve, route,
peer, federate, query, mirror, exchange, publish, or otherwise cooperate with a
declared interface.

Recognition is not permission, endorsement, legal certification, ethical
certification, commercial certification, or political approval.

## Interface-Specific Consent

Interface-specific consent means that cooperation in one interface does not
imply cooperation in another.

A root may cooperate for DNS and decline Fediverse. A root may federate socially
and decline WireGuard. A root may exchange mail and decline directory access.
Each interface stands on its own.

## CEAS

`CEAS` means Civic Email Alias System.

CEAS is an email-affordance mechanism by which a county implementation may issue
and operate civic email aliases for qualified participants, especially under the
`CURRENT_RESIDENT` affordance.

CEAS is not ordinary lifetime email, government email, a voting system, a legal
identity credential, a marketing list, or proof that a person is morally,
politically, commercially, legally, or ethically endorsed by the network.

A CEAS implementation may support:

- participant-facing civic email aliases;
- internal mailbox or account routing;
- alias lifecycle and rotation;
- sender authorization;
- validation of active alias status;
- email-based civic reachability;
- selected durable attestation records.

A county root may expose an Email / CEAS cooperation interface without requiring
all other county roots to implement CEAS in the same way.

## SASE

`SASE` means self-addressed stamped envelope.

Within a `CURRENT_RESIDENT` process, a SASE may be used as evidence of voluntary
participation intent and postal reachability. The participant assembles the
envelope, pays postage, and requests access to the civic email alias process.

SASE is a process component, not a universal requirement for every civic
affordance.

## Local Attribution

Local attribution is visible credit for a local contributor, operator, business,
charity, professional, technical group, or other locally grounded actor.

Attribution recognizes contribution. It is not a purchased advertising
entitlement.

## Local Commerce

Civic Infrastructure is not required to be free of cost.

A local actor may pay for implementation, hosting, administration, consulting,
technical support, maintenance, or contribution. Local commerce does not create
entitlement to cooperative recognition.

## Anti-Capture

Anti-capture is the policy boundary that prevents Civic Infrastructure from
becoming a scalable surface for outside corporations, remote advertisers,
centralized sponsors, astroturf operators, or actors without local standing.

Anti-capture does not mean anti-commerce. It means local commerce must remain
locally grounded and cannot buy cooperative recognition.

## Isolation

Isolation is the ordinary consequence of declined cooperation.

If roots decline DNS recognition, federation, peering, routing, directory
exchange, mail exchange, trust publication, or attestation mirroring, the
implementation may remain online but outside that cooperation layer.

Isolation is not central punishment.

## Diagnostics

Diagnostics means exposure, inspection, classification, comparison, and record
discipline before solution.

The model wants to surface conditions. Acceptance, refusal, cooperation,
forking, obstruction, attack, capture, failure, incompatibility, and local
disagreement are all diagnostic signals.

## Non-Authority Posture

Civic Affordance Diagnostics does not use diagnostic surfaces to govern,
adjudicate, represent, enforce, punish, regulate, certify, or replace existing
institutions.

The model defines vocabulary, surfaces, interfaces, and evidence discipline.
