# Model Vocabulary Addendum

Status: Draft  
Repository: `civic-affordance-model`  
Scope: Civic Affordances Diagnostics

## Purpose

This addendum records vocabulary needed for the cooperation-interface model.

It should be read alongside the diagnostic families and diagnostic surface qualification taxonomy.

## WAST

`WAST` is emphasized capitalization meaning complex and extensive.

It is not an acronym unless later defined as one.

The subject domain is WAST because the civic, technical, social, economic, legal, administrative, and evidentiary conditions around affected-status infrastructure are complex and extensive.

## Model, Not Product

Civic Affordance Diagnostics is a model.

It is not a product, SaaS platform, vendor package, franchise, centralized application, or single-stack deployment.

A reference implementation may exist. A deployment playbook may exist. A county root may publish operational choices.

Those do not convert the model into a product or make local implementation choices mandatory for other counties.

## Reference Implementation

A reference implementation is one implementation of the model.

It may demonstrate choices such as DNS, DANE, Hubzilla, WireGuard, LDAP, Email, CEAS, IPFS, Ansible, Proxmox, LXD, or other technologies.

A reference implementation does not impose prerequisites on other county roots.

## County Root

A county root is the local owner-operator or operator group that controls a declared county-state surface.

The county root is responsible for the interfaces it exposes and the local arrangements it makes.

A county root does not control other county roots.

## County-State Surface

A county-state surface is a declared civic infrastructure surface for a specific county and state.

The surface may exist under any domain controlled by the contributor.

Examples:

```text
kane-il.us
orange-ca.ankerpoint-sawmill.com
orange-ca.example.org
orange-ca.anotherexample.net
```

The surface is not a monopoly claim over the county.

## Cooperation Interface

A cooperation interface is a declared technical or administrative boundary through which one root may cooperate with another.

Examples:

- public ingress;
- DNS;
- Fediverse;
- directory / LDAP;
- Email / CEAS;
- WireGuard;
- DANE;
- attestation / durable publication.

## Interface-Specific Consent

Interface-specific consent means that cooperation in one interface does not imply cooperation in another.

A root may cooperate for DNS and decline Fediverse.

A root may federate socially and decline WireGuard.

A root may exchange mail and decline directory access.

Each interface stands on its own.

## Local Attribution

Local attribution is visible credit for a local contributor, operator, business, charity, professional, technical group, or other locally grounded actor.

Attribution recognizes contribution. It is not a purchased advertising entitlement.

## Anti-Capture

Anti-capture is the policy boundary that prevents Civic Infrastructure from becoming a scalable surface for outside corporations, remote advertisers, centralized sponsors, astroturf operators, or actors without local standing.

Anti-capture does not mean anti-commerce.

It means local commerce must remain locally grounded and cannot buy cooperative recognition.

## Isolation

Isolation is the ordinary consequence of declined cooperation.

If roots decline DNS recognition, federation, peering, routing, directory exchange, mail exchange, trust publication, or attestation mirroring, the implementation may remain online but outside that cooperation layer.

Isolation is not central punishment.

## Diagnostics

Diagnostics means exposure, inspection, classification, comparison, and record discipline before solution.

The model wants to surface conditions.

Acceptance, refusal, cooperation, forking, obstruction, attack, capture, failure, incompatibility, and local disagreement are all diagnostic signals.

## Wiring / Purposing Separation

The wiring is not the purposing.

Civic Infrastructure may route, resolve, federate, validate, query, publish, or attest. Those functions do not make the infrastructure responsible for every purpose pursued by local actors using the infrastructure.

The model does not issue general legal, ethical, political, commercial, or institutional certification.
